Immune watches this Mac for signs of attack and tells you, or your AI coding agent, what it found and what it could not see.

This installer places two things, owned by the system:

  • the core, the command immune, at /usr/local/bin/immune
  • the optional menu-bar app at /Applications/Immune.app

It runs no script and starts nothing. Immune does not watch this Mac until you turn it on; the last page says how.
